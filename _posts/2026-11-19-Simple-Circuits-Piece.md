## Party Hat

This hat uses circuits and a homemade button to display four LED lights. My motivation for creating this piece is that I love to party and 
I love fun festive colors. They make me happy.

Material list: Hat, Felt, two pairs of snaps, conductive thread, embroidery floss, needle, LED lights, coin cell battery, coin cell battery holder

One tip to my past self would be to sew parts with regular thread first and then go in with the conductive thread so that the conductive thread can be tight and 
stitched into the right places. Another tip I would give to my past self would be to draw an outline with a pen before sewing letters so I don't have to
resew certain stitches.
