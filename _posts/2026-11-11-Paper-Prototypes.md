# Final Project Paper Prototypes 
  

---


For this assignment, I created a paper prototype to help model my final projects. The paper prototypes will help me figure out if there are any issues in 
circuitry or overall structure before I begin the actual building process. Below are the titles and descriptions of my project. 

## Flashy Wristband
This wristband displays simultaneous but different light patterns, and the lights get brighter as you get warmer, using the temperature sensor.

## Party Hat
This hat uses circuits and a homemade switch to display lights, emphasizing the festive nature of the hat. 

## Festive Glove
This glove uses a button and light sensor to turn on a song through the buzzer as well as some LED lights. It works so that when you clap,
you press the button and cover the light sensor, causing the song to play and the LED lights to turn on.
