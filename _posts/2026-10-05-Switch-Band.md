## Switch Band
  

---


In this E-textiles assignment, we had to create a switch band with two circuit breaks so that when they were closed, an LED would light up. For this project, I decided to use 
two snaps to close one circuit break and a paperclip to close the second circuit break. I decided to include 2 LEDs to add more creativity and fun to my project.

![Babt Seal](https://pinc1-tech.github.io/assets/img/IMG_3417.jpeg)

One tip I would give to others before starting this assignment for the first time is to use tape instead of thread for holding the components on to the felt before
sewing it with conductive thread. This saves time and makes the process a lot easier :)
