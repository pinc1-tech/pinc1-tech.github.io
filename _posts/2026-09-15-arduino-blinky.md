## Arduino Blinky Assignment 
### Turning Lights On And Off with Arduino  

---


In this Arduino coding assignment, the expectation was to turn each of the Lilypad LED lights on depending on the multiple of seconds that have passed. For example, I had to turn on the yellow light after a multiple of 5 seconds had passed, and turn on the blue light after a multiple 8 seconds had passed. Only one light can be turned on at a time and should immediately turn off when the next light turns on. 

This is an image of my output:
(https://pinc1-tech.github.io/assets/img/unnamed.jpg)


One tip I would give to myself before starting this assignment is that it truly is important to write code little by little and test it each time. It can be confusing to write a lot of code and not understand where the error is happening and how it affects everything else, so it is nice to go step by step.
