Rought draft of pitch: Hello everyone, today I will be sharing my project proposal for the final project. I love to party, so I decided to create my project in inspiration of that. My project is called Party Gear and includes three things that would amplify the party experience. The first object I have is the Flashy Wristband. The Flashy Wristband displays simultaneous but different behavior the whole time, but its brightness changes based on how hard you are partying (how hot you are). The Party Hat has a piece of felt that says PARTY (my homemade button) which once it is clipped on with metal snaps, festive lights turn on. Finally, the Festive Glove plays a sound and displays lights when you clap (you press a button on the glove and cover the light sensor). With this party gear, everyone at the party will think you are cool and fall in love with you.
