## Detecting Ghosts With Temperature and Light Sensor   

---

In this E-Textiles assignment, we used a light and temperature sensor to turn on red LED lights when it was dark and cold enough. In all other cases, the 
LED lights had to be off. This way, we can detect ghosts whenever it gets too cold and dark!

![Baby Seal](https://pinc1-tech.github.io/assets/img/unnamed-6.jpg)


If I had to give a tip to my past self when starting this project, I would set high values for my temperature and light sensor just to see if the LEDs turn on,
and then change the values back down.
